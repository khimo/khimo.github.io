01Overview

BeoLiving Intelligence is the first plug & play smart-home controller for Bang & Olufsen — the successor to the BeoLink Gateway, born from the B&O × khimo partnership that dates back to 2005. It expands how your B&O products work together and lets them interact with the rest of your home, all through the beautifully simple BeoLiving App on your phone or tablet.

It works straight out of the box in Essential mode — easy to install and configure yourself. Upgrade once to PRO for a full professional smart-home solution.
