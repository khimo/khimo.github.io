Semantic search across the BeoLiving Intelligence guides, FAQ, troubleshooting and supported-systems documentation. It matches meaning, not just keywords — ask in your own words (e.g. “dim the lights when the sun sets”). Runs entirely in your browser: a small AI model and a precomputed index are downloaded once, then everything happens on your device. No Google, no server, no tracking.
